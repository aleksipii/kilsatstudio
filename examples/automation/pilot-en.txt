ON MY OWN TIME — new design pilot: Just a tiny job
Duration: 50 seconds. Location: depot break room.
Characters: Aino, maintenance worker; Rane, supervisor.
[0–3] Aino puts on her coat. Rane slides the keys across the table.
[3–8] RANE: It's just a tiny job. Take your own car. We need to keep costs down.
[8–13] AINO: Whose costs? My car hasn't joined the company savings programme.
[13–18] RANE: Stop counting every mile. We need flexibility and a sense of shared responsibility.
[18–24] AINO: I've got both. And Kilsat. I'll log the trip so we can remember it the same way.
[24–29] Aino shows a separately obtained real Kilsat – Ajopäiväkirja screen. No invented features.
[29–34] RANE: I'd remember it as free. That would make the paperwork much lighter for us.
[34–39] AINO: Then we're taking different routes. I'm driving this one. You're driving your point home.
[39–44] RANE: What if I write that we agreed in a spirit of goodwill?
[44–49] AINO: Go ahead. Include the distance. Goodwill doesn't go in the fuel tank.
[49–50] Aino picks up the keys. Rane looks into his empty coffee cup. Cut to black.
